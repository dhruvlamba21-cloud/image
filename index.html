ABOUT THE IDEA

Old-world
texture.

Da Vinci Portrait is a playful digital atelier built around one idea: a modern photograph can feel completely different when it is treated like an old study. The visual language is inspired by Renaissance drawing, manuscript paper and the restraint of monochrome ink.
